# AI answer check

## Before you ask

Get the original company results release from the exchange or company site. Attach or provide that document to your assistant.

## Prompt template

Summarise the attached full-year results announcement for [company] for an everyday investor. Give me exactly 5 bullet points. Each bullet must state one specific fact (a number, a time period or an outlook statement) and the page it comes from. Do not tell me whether to buy, sell or hold.

## Check five claims

1. Take each claim separately and find the cited page in the original release.
2. Find the exact line or table entry that supports it.
3. Check the number and the period. Make sure the figure belongs to the period the answer names.
4. Mark each claim:
   - **Supported:** the source says the same thing, with the same number and period.
   - **Unsupported:** the source gives a conflicting figure or detail.
   - **Not found:** you cannot locate evidence for the claim in the source.
5. Look for relevant information the summary left out, especially the outlook.

## Red flags

- The period is wrong or unclear.
- A number does not appear in the document.
- A one-line caveat is followed by confident, detailed figures.
- The summary leaves out the outlook.
- It mixes adjusted and reported figures. Adjusted figures exclude specified items; reported figures include them.

## What needs a human

Read the full outlook and consider business context, comparisons with prior periods or peers, and your own circumstances and objectives. This checklist is general information, not advice. Consider speaking to a licensed adviser.
